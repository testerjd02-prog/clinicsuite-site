Common questions

Do I need internet?

No. The PC app and your phone connection work without internet. You only need it to upload a backup to Google Drive and for remote support.

Where is my patient data stored?

On your clinic computer. Backups go to your own Google Drive account. There is no ClinicSuite server holding your data.

Can you see my patients' records?

No. I only connect to your PC for support when you open the support screen and allow it.

Will it use my clinic's letterhead?

Yes. Your clinic name and details are set up for you, and every prescription prints with them.

What if something stops working?

Message me on WhatsApp. I connect to your PC remotely and fix it.

What happens after the free trial?

You choose a plan and I activate your licence. If you don't want it, I remove it and nothing is owed.
